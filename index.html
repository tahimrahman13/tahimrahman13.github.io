Fish and companions

Keeping fish and birds feels like having a friend to share moments with every day. Nobody inspired me. I found this interest myself.

I have kept goldfish, angelfish and molly fish in a bowl, and right now I keep zebrafish. I have also kept quail birds many times, but my family does not want pets, so they never stayed long.

The plan

After my annual examination I will buy a ten-gallon tank setup for my zebrafish. If they do not survive long, I will get six guppies and some mystery snails.

The pigeon who left

A pigeon once came into our house and my grandmother caged it. I freed it, and it came back every day for six or seven months. I only fed it rice, so in the end it went back to its other owner.
